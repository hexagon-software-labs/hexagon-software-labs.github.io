Frequently asked questions

What does Hexagon do?

We design, build and run software: web and mobile apps, cloud backends and the integrations they need.

Can you build software for my business?

Yes. Email us what you need, and we reply with our questions and a first plan.

Which technologies do you use?

TypeScript with NestJS and React, Go for fast services, PostgreSQL and Redis, all shipped in Docker containers.

Do you build in Persian?

Yes. We design right-to-left, Persian-first screens with Jalali dates, and we work in English too.

Where is the software hosted?

On servers close to its users. For users in Iran, that means servers inside Iran.

What is Kafeban?

Our own product for cafés and restaurants in Iran. Read more on kafeban.ir.
