How we work

We build each product for the place it runs. Kafeban is Persian first, its dates are Jalali, its prices are exact to the rial, and its orders run on servers inside Iran.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hexagon?

Hexagon Software Labs is a software company. We build our own products and run them as a service; Kafeban, for cafés and restaurants in Iran, is the first.

Who is Kafeban for?

Cafés and restaurants, from one counter to several halls with waiters and a kitchen printer.

Does Kafeban issue tax invoices?

Yes. Invoices go to the Moadian tax system, and a refund goes as a return invoice.

Which devices does Kafeban run on?

Any phone, tablet or PC with a browser. The staff app also installs on Android.

What does Kafeban do when the internet is down?

Waiters and the counter keep ordering on the café's network. The QR menu stays readable and shows a button to call a waiter.

Where is Kafeban's data kept?

On servers inside Iran, with encrypted backups every day.

Can Kafeban print receipts in Persian?

Yes, on standard receipt and kitchen printers.

How do we start with Kafeban?

Email us. We set up your menu, tables and staff with you and show your team how it works.
